# Medivista Multispeciality Hospital — patient FAQ

Sample data for the clinic demo. Replace every answer with the hospital's real details before go-live.

## Location and contact
- Address: Plot 12, Sector 62, Noida, Uttar Pradesh 201309. About 5 minutes from Noida Sector 62 metro station.
- Main entrance and Emergency are both at Gate 1. Wheelchairs and help desk staff are available at Gate 1.
- Appointments, rescheduling and cancellation: call this same number any time; the assistant handles it 24x7.

## Timings
- OPD (outpatient clinics): Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 8 PM. Sunday 10 AM to 1 PM, General Medicine only.
- Each doctor has their own OPD days and hours; the assistant checks them live.
- Emergency, pharmacy and ambulance: open 24 hours, all 7 days.
- Laboratory: 7 AM to 9 PM daily. Fasting blood tests are best done between 7 and 10 AM.
- Inpatient visiting hours: 11 AM to 1 PM and 5 PM to 7 PM. One attendant pass per patient. Children under 12 are not allowed in the ICU.

## Before your visit
- Please arrive 15 minutes before your appointment time for registration.
- First visit: bring a photo ID and any previous prescriptions and reports. Registration is free.
- Consultation fees are paid at the reception before the consultation. Cash, UPI, debit and credit cards are accepted.
- One follow-up visit with the same doctor within 7 days is free.
- There is no charge for cancelling or rescheduling an appointment.

## Insurance
- Cashless hospitalisation is available with most major insurance companies and TPAs. Bring the insurance card or policy copy and a photo ID.
- OPD consultations are usually not cashless; keep the bills for reimbursement as per your policy.
- For a specific insurer or TPA, the insurance desk (Block A, Ground Floor) can confirm.

## Tests and reports
- Blood tests: walk in to the lab, no appointment needed.
- X-ray is walk-in. Ultrasound, CT scan and MRI need a slot booked at the Radiology desk (Block A, 1st Floor).
- Routine test reports are ready within 24 hours and are sent by SMS link; they can also be collected at the lab counter.
- Health check-up packages (basic, executive, cardiac, women's) are booked at the front desk; come fasting for 10 to 12 hours.

## Facilities
- Pharmacy: Block A, Ground Floor, 24x7.
- Cafeteria: Block D, Ground Floor, 7 AM to 10 PM.
- Parking: basement, first 30 minutes free, then paid.
- ATM and drinking water near Gate 1.

## What the assistant cannot do
- It does not give medical advice, diagnoses, test interpretations or prescriptions; a doctor does that in the consultation.
- It cannot book radiology slots, surgeries, health packages or admissions; the front desk handles those.
